Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how Startup Intelligence handles information when you visit our website or submit a project enquiry. We aim to collect only information that helps us respond to enquiries, understand project needs, and operate the website.

1. Who we are

Startup Intelligence is an independent digital project offering website development and related digital services. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. If you have a privacy question or want to request access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, contact us through our Contact page or email startupintelligence1@gmail.com.

2. Information you may provide

If you submit our project enquiry form, you may provide your name, business name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, business type and location, website link, selected services, information about your business and customers, project requirements, current tools, desired outcomes, marketing needs, budget, timing, decision-making details, and any other information you choose to include. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identity numbers, or other sensitive information that is not needed for an enquiry.

3. How we use information

  • To review and respond to project enquiries.
  • To understand your business needs and prepare relevant questions, recommendations, estimates, or proposals.
  • To contact you using the details and contact preference you provide.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the website and enquiry process.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and handle disputes when necessary.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use enquiry details to send unrelated marketing campaigns unless we have an appropriate basis and comply with applicable requirements.

4. Form provider and connected spreadsheet

Our enquiry form is provided by Tally. When you submit it, the information you enter is processed by Tally under its own terms and privacy policy. We may use a connected Google Sheets spreadsheet to organise and review enquiries; information submitted through the form may therefore be copied to that spreadsheet and processed by Google under its own terms and privacy policy. Access to enquiry records should be limited to people who need them to handle requests. See Tally’s privacy information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

5. Hosting, logs, and browser storage

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure may process technical information, such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and browser or device information, to deliver and protect the service. Review GitHub’s Privacy Statement and the GitHub Pages documentation.

The website uses browser local storage to remember your display-theme preference under the key si-theme. This setting stays in your browser until you clear site data and is not sent to Startup Intelligence by the theme feature. We do not intentionally include third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or session-replay scripts in the website code reviewed for this policy. This may change if new tools are added.

6. Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed to operate the enquiry process, respond to you, protect our rights and systems, or comply with law. This may involve Tally, Google Sheets/Google, GitHub Pages, and other providers only if they are later added to the process. These providers have their own terms and privacy practices. We do not control how third-party services independently handle information.

7. Storage, retention, and security

We intend to keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a potential project, maintain necessary business records, resolve issues, or meet legal requirements. We will review records and remove them when they are no longer needed where practical. Copies held by service providers or in backups may follow those providers’ retention processes.

We use reasonable care to limit access to enquiry information, but no website, transmission method, or storage service can be guaranteed completely secure. Do not send confidential credentials or unnecessary sensitive information through the form.

8. Your choices and requests

You can choose what information to provide, although we may not be able to respond fully without a way to contact you and enough detail about your request. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted. We may need to verify the request and may retain some information where lawfully required. Information already held by Tally, Google, GitHub, or another provider may also need to be managed under that provider’s own processes.

9. Children

This website and enquiry form are intended for businesses and general audiences, not for collecting personal information from children. Please do not submit information about a child unless it is necessary and you have the authority to do so.

10. External links

Links to external websites are provided for reference. Their content, security, and privacy practices are controlled by their respective operators, not Startup Intelligence.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services, form, or data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. We should review this policy before adding accounts, payments, analytics, advertising, file uploads, or other data-collection features.

12. Important note

This policy describes our intended current practices in plain language. It is not legal advice and does not guarantee compliance with every law that may apply. We will review the policy and our actual workflows as the project develops.
