Frequently asked questions

Before you get started.

Details that affect scope, pricing and delivery should be confirmed in writing for each project.

Are the collection previews finished templates?

The current previews are illustrative design concepts to help explain possible directions. They are not presented as live demos or completed client websites.

Why are prices not listed yet?

Package pricing has not been configured. The scope and price should be agreed in a written proposal before any payment is requested.

Can you build a login, dashboard or AI feature?

These may require a backend, database, trusted authentication, and external services. Availability, feasibility, security and ongoing costs must be reviewed before they are promised.

Can I request a custom quote?

Yes. Use the custom project page to review the information needed for a quote. A secure inquiry submission flow still needs a backend before it can be accepted online.
